# Phase 1 — the likeness gate

> **Human-only gate.** The coding agent cannot run, judge, certify, or fill in any
> result below. It cannot look at a model and tell you whether it reads as Crash.
> Three named friends answer this, cold, or it is not answered.

Spec §9.3. This gate exists because everyone can already picture Crash, so every
deviation reads as error rather than style — and that bar lands precisely where the
artist is greenest. Running it early is the cheapest insurance in the plan: it stops
six months of environment work being built around a mascot that reads as
"off-brand orange thing".

## What this gate blocks

**Level-art production only.** It does not block code, level authoring in graybox,
systems work, or the Island Cut release. Nothing on this page gates anything except
the start of real environment and character art.

## Prerequisites

- The Crash model, **untextured**. No fur, no colour, no logo, no pose that gives it
  away. Grey geometry only.
- Three friends who have **not** seen it in progress and have not been told what it
  is meant to be. If someone has watched it being modelled, they cannot be one of
  the three.
- Show it cold. Do not say "guess who this is", do not mention the project, do not
  put it next to anything Crash-related.

## Pass criterion

**All three name him unprompted.** Two out of three is a fail. "Something like
Crash", "a bandicoot", or "that PlayStation fox" are not names — record them
verbatim and judge honestly; the temptation to round them up is the reason this
page asks for their exact words.

Result: **PASS / FAIL / NOT RUN**
