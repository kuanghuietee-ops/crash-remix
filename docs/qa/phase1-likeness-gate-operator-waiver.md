## Risk accepted

The operator accepts that Crash may fail the required blind-recognition test, and that
the environment, enemy, rideable and boss art unlocked below may need rework afterwards.

**The exposure is now the whole Phase 1 art surface, and that is worth stating plainly.**
Between the two directives, all four Island Cut environments and the full character batch
are being built against a mascot no outside observer has identified unprompted. That is
precisely the sunk-cost shape §9.3 exists to prevent. It is the operator's call, recorded
here rather than softened. If Crash's proportions change, the palette, scale
relationships and shared trim bands of the environment art are all in the blast radius.

## Scope boundary

This waiver unlocks only:

- the Beach/jungle environment package: an original atlas and trim sheet for the kit,
  and the material, UV and `.import` changes needed to put those textures on it;
- environment art and level scenery for `wr1_n_sanity_beach`, `wr1_boulders`,
  `wr1_hog_wild`, `warp_room_1` and `wr1_papu_papu`, including new kit pieces and
  palette entries those environments need, and the scene edits that place them;
- original skink and carnivorous-plant enemy art;
- one unshipped practice quadruped rig;
- original hog rideable art;
- original Papu Papu boss art;
- the two triangle bands the operator chose: `kit_piece` 100–2,000, chosen against the
  measured kit range of 100–1,564; and `rideable` 6,000–10,000, approved before the
  first hog;
- the integration, previews, tests, budget checks, Android build and combined release
  needed to inspect those assets.

The practice quadruped remains unbudgeted and must not ship as game content.

It does **not** unlock the temple-ruins or any other Phase 2 §9.4 kit, Phase 2 content,
baked lighting as a separate package, the Island Cut acceptance gate, or Gate F and
Gate F2. Those remain in force. A later operator decision is required before any of them
begins.
